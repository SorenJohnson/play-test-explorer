Start Game:
Put End Game card on the bottom of the Event Deck
Adjust each resource price (1d20)
Draw Contract cards (2 + Num Players)
Draw 4 cards for the pool
Deal 3 cards to each player
Choose a Corporation (reverse starting order)

Turn:
Spend up to 2 cards to take Actions (can swap cards from pool with hand)
Draw back up to hand size (3 cards)
Draw 1 event cards (If indicated, adjust PWR price by active player’s rate)

Actions:
Play Building(s)
Sell Resource
Fulfill Contract

Play Building(s)
Must play all buildings together as one Action (total the costs)
Can discard cards to reduce a resource cost by one per card
Buy deficit costs (of all buildings) not covered by current resource rates
Pay deficit times price
Increase price by deficit

Sell Resource:
Requires matching type on card
Take money equal to resource rate times price
Reduce price by rate

Fulfill Contract:
Requires Contract icon on card OR discarding two cards
Reduce rate by cost (cannot go negative)
Take contract card
Draw new contract card into empty slot

Events:
Draw Pool Card
News Bulletin (draw from News deck)
Patent Auction
Power Bill
Debt Collection
Futures Trade
End Game

Patent Auction:
Reveal top Patent
In turn order, bid or pass (passing is permanent)
Continue bidding until all but one pass
Bids need to be in increments of $5
Bids are spent with debt

Power Bill
Players with positive PWR get rate times price in money
Players with negative PWR get rate times price in debt

Debt Collection
Can pay off debt in increments of $10
Increase debt by $1 per $10 owed (minus total value of Contracts)

Futures Settlement:
Players with negative resources buy at market rate (as debt)
Increase price by total negative resource rates

End Game:
Do a final Power Bill
Do a final Futures Settlement
Player with the most money wins
